📖 The Mom Test Cheatsheet

🚨 Defusing Bad Data

1. Compliments: Defuse by deflecting back to their life: “Thanks! But how do you handle it today without this?”

2. Fluff (“I usually / I might”): Anchor to the past: “When was the exact last time that happened?”

3. Feature Requests: Dig for the root problem: “Why do you want that? What would that allow you to do?”

✅ Golden Questions

  • “Why do you bother doing that?”
  • “What are the implications if that doesn't get done?”
  • “Talk me through the last time that happened.”
  • “What else have you tried?”
  • “Where does the money or budget come from?”

❌ Deadly Questions (Never Ask)

  • “Do you think this is a good idea?”
  • “Would you buy a product that did X?”
  • “How much would you pay for this?”
